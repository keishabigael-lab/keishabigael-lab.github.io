About me

I'm Lakeisha Assyifa Abigaile, but everyone calls me Keisha.

I'm a double-degree student at BINUS University, studying DKV (Visual Communication Design) and Sistem Informasi (Information Systems) at the same time. Design trains my eye. Systems train my thinking. I like using both to turn culture and everyday places into friendly, useful visuals.
